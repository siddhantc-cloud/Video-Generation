## Canonical Manual Style block

Paste this text into **Style Settings → Manual Style**. Keep it unchanged across production candidates unless the shot record names and explains an override.

> Grounded photoreal mythological epic with a reverent, human-scale register. Color: sun-faded warm amber, muted earths, restrained saturation, dense true blacks with readable shadow detail, controlled highlights, subtle gold bloom. Lighting: one motivated natural source; preserve the approved keyframe's direction and time of day; sky and water provide soft natural fill; faces keep dimensional shadow. Texture: Fine Film, fine natural grain, mild halation around practical highlights only; tactile skin, fur, woven cloth, hand-worked metal, wet stone and salt water. Motion: physically plausible weight, contact and inertia; natural motion blur. Performance: quiet, contained, reverent; small eye movements, natural breathing, economical gestures. Composition: story-first, clean silhouette and readable subject separation. Sacred inscriptions, captions, financial copy and logos are added in post.

Manual Style length: **899 characters**, below the observed 2,000-character ceiling.

## Controlled overrides

- Tender Rama/squirrel close-ups may lower contrast and use softer optics, but must retain the same skin, costume, fur, material, and color families.
- Comic vanara beats may increase motion energy, but not switch to cartoon rendering or broad theatrical acting.
- Mist/overcast bridge shots may cool the environment while skin and sacred warmth stay perceptually connected to the global palette.
- Every override belongs in the shot's companion record before generation; an unrecorded override is a continuity error.
