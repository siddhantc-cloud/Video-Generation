## Film

- Working title: The Squirrel in Ram Setu
- Brand: ZebPay
- Product idea: SIP / systematic investing
- Format: 9:16 vertical social video
- Language: Indian-English narration
- Structure: narrated mythological story → modern investing parallel → ZebPay sponsor close

## Audience takeaway

You do not need to predict the perfect market moment. Small investments made consistently can compound
into meaningful results, just as the squirrel’s repeated pebbles contributed to the bridge.

## Story summary

Rama’s army is building Ram Setu with enormous boulders. A tiny squirrel contributes pebbles, is mocked,
and is then recognized by Rama for showing up without being asked. The story becomes a parallel for SIP:
the repeated contribution matters more than the size or the day on which it was made.

## Creative constraints

- Rama must be depicted reverently and consistently.
- The squirrel’s three stripes and character identity must remain stable.
- Avoid model-rendered text and logos; finish them in post.
- Keep narrator identity continuous across the film.
- Preserve story clarity over visual spectacle.
- The claim “India’s oldest crypto exchange” must be confirmed by the responsible human/legal reviewer
  before final delivery; its presence in the script is not itself approval.

## Definition of done

The film is done only when all planned shots have approved selects, the final continuous VO/music/SFX
mix is locked, graphics and captions are correct, creative and brand/legal reviews are recorded, the
vertical master passes technical QC, and the human owner approves the final export.
