## Narration

An army of vanaras was building Ram Setu, boulder by boulder.

At the edge of the shore, a tiny squirrel carried one pebble at a time.

The others laughed. What difference could something so small make?

But Rama saw what they missed.

The squirrel kept showing up, whether the tide was calm or rough. And with every return, another gap
was filled.
