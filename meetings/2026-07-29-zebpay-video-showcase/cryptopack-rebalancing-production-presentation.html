Why review the encode?

The first cut was not the final cut

Reviewing the actual encoded v001 found concrete blockers that source timelines alone could not certify.

v001 · blocked

  • Unsafe or misleading source-screen copy remained visible.
  • A focus label needed safer product wording.
  • Some caption groups entered after their spoken first word.

v002 · keeper

  • Unsafe screenshot copy was deliberately masked.
  • Focus changed to “Allocations or tokens may update.”
  • First-word caption onsets were corrected.

What we learned

A reusable system for product films

01 · TRUTH

Let the product be the hero.

Use source screenshots wherever UI, controls or claims must be exact.

02 · CLOCK

Lock one voice performance first.

It gives motion, captions and music one coherent human rhythm.

03 · CONTROL

Animate deterministic surfaces.

Exact text and repeatable timing belong in code and post—not generated imagery.

04 · PROOF

Judge what the audience receives.

Encoded frames and measured audio are the real approval evidence.
