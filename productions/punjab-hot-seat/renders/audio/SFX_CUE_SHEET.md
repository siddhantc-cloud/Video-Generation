# SFX Cue Sheet — "Punjab Di Kursi" (full film, 50.5s)

> **Purpose:** spec for hand-placed, frame-accurate sound effects baked into the master. SFX are **manually sourced** (free/CC-licensed: Mixkit, Pixabay, Freesound CC0 — small direct-download files), NOT AI-generated. SFX sit **over the existing music bed** ("Monkeys Spinning Monkeys"); levels set so comedic hits cut through.
>
> **Master timeline:** Act 1 `0:00–10.0` · Act 2 `10.0–25.1` · Act 3 `25.1–40.3` · Act 4 `40.3–50.5`.
> **Style:** classic 2D-cartoon / So Sorry comedic foley — whooshes, boings, thuds, dust-poofs, sparkles, party hits. Punchy and clean, not realistic.
> **Timestamps** are master-timeline and ~±0.3s; exact frame is confirmed at bake by extracting the action frame.
> **Workflow:** download each file → `audio/sfx/<id>.<ext>` → I place at `t`, set gain, short fade, mux. Files I can't hear — the **suggested sound** + filename is the contract.

## Sourcing & licensing
- **Pixabay SFX** (no-attribution) & **Mixkit SFX** (free license) — direct-downloadable small files; first choice.
- **Freesound.org** — filter to **CC0** for no-attribution; great for specific cartoon foley.
- Avoid anything not clearly free/CC for a clean-rights showcase. Log each file's source/license in `audio/sfx/CREDITS.txt`.

## Mixing rules (at bake)
- SFX over the music bed; **duck music −3 to −5 dB** for ~0.4s under the loudest hits (A1-04, A1-05, A3-06, A4-05).
- Each SFX: 10–20 ms fade-in, short fade-out; trim to the beat; no clipping (keep peaks ≤ −1 dBFS).
- Confirm exact frame per cue by extracting the action frame before placing.

## Open question for the user
- Keep the current **music bed under the SFX** (recommended — fuller), or do an **SFX-only** pass first to judge them dry? Default: keep music, duck under hits.
