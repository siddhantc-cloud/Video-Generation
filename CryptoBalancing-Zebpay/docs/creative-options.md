# Creative and asset options

## Chosen direction

The first video is a 45-second **Sell + Explain** portrait product promo. It uses ZebPay’s real app
screens as the evidence and a clean deterministic motion system to make allocation drift,
realignment, transparency, and control immediately legible.

## Script options

1. **Sell + Explain — produced.** Balances persuasion, product explanation, transparency, and CTA.
2. **Product Walkthrough — documented only.** Starts in the interface and explains the feature in
   screen order.
3. **Conversion Ad — documented only.** Uses a faster emotional hook and earlier CTA while retaining
   the essential fees/taxes disclosure.

The exact scripts are under `scripts/`.

## UI asset ladder

1. **Fresh original app captures.** Preferred if the owner supplies current PNGs or supervised
   authenticated capture access before picture lock.
2. **Original images extracted from the PDF.** Current production baseline. The PDF contains four
   unique portrait captures at roughly 1000×2048, sufficient for a 1080×1920 master with restrained
   scaling and cropping.
3. **Deterministic UI reconstruction.** Limited to exact overlays such as allocation bars,
   highlights, tap rings, labels, and transitions. Do not redraw an entire authoritative screen.
4. **ChatGPT ImageGen or Canvas restoration experiment.** Optional later comparison for decorative
   material or local cleanup. Any result must be reviewed beside the real screenshot and must not
   add, rewrite, or imply product content.

## Visual treatment

- ZebPay blue-violet gradients, pale blue-white canvas, dark navy type, and restrained teal/yellow
  status accents sampled from the source screens.
- The phone screen remains the hero. Motion graphics clarify the product rather than surrounding it
  with generic crypto spectacle.
- Market drift is illustrative and labelled as such. It is not presented as a real transaction,
  forecast, or return.
- One visual idea reveals at a time, with eased bar movement, soft depth, clean crop changes, and
  deliberate landing frames.
- The enable screen owns the final frame. The disable screen is only a brief proof of user control.

## Audio treatment

- One playful, enthusiastic female neutral-English ElevenLabs voice is generated first. Additional
  auditions happen only after owner feedback.
- “ZebPay” is pronounced “Zeb-pay”; “CryptoPack” is spoken as “Crypto Pack.”
- Music is a light, playful, rights-cleared technology bed mixed below speech.
- Small tap, swish, and confirmation sounds support visible UI motion.
- Captions remain useful when the in-app video starts muted.

## Deferred alternatives

- A current live-app recapture pass.
- A carefully reviewed image-restoration comparison.
- A different ElevenLabs female voice after owner feedback.
- A 30-second conversion derivative after the primary 45-second version is approved.
- Landscape or square derivatives only after the portrait campaign cut is locked.
