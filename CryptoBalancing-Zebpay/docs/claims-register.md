# Claims register

## Approved working language

- Market movements can cause a CryptoPack to drift from its target allocation.
- Rebalancing can trim coins that have become overweight and buy coins that are underweight.
- Rebalancing realigns a CryptoPack with its target allocation or intended strategy.
- ZebPay’s research-backed process may update allocations or tokens as market conditions change.
- Users can review rebalancing history and transaction details.
- Standard rebalancing fees and applicable taxes apply.
- Users can enable or disable rebalancing from the relevant Pack settings/history experience.
- Rebalancing may help manage risk, but it does not guarantee profit or prevent loss.

## Prohibited or approval-required language

| Phrase or implication | Status | Safer replacement |
|---|---|---|
| “Optimal strategy” | Do not use | “Intended strategy” or “target allocation” |
| “Protect your long-term returns” | Do not use | “Keep the Pack aligned as conditions change” |
| “Protect your portfolio” | Legal/product approval required | “Help manage allocation drift” |
| “Experts continuously analyse” | Do not use without evidence | “The research-backed process may update…” |
| “Boost performance” | Do not use without approval | Describe the actual allocation/token update |
| Guaranteed profit, growth, or loss prevention | Prohibited | State that rebalancing does not guarantee profit |
| Unlabelled fictional values or transactions | Prohibited | Mark explanatory animation “Illustrative” |

Final product, legal, regulatory, and brand approval belongs to ZebPay. This register controls the
working cut until that approval is recorded.
