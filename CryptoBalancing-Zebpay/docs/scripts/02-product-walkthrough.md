## Voice-over

Here’s how CryptoPack Rebalancing works.

When market prices change, the coins in a CryptoPack can move away from their target percentages.
Rebalancing automatically trims coins that are overweight and buys coins that are underweight,
bringing the Pack back towards its target allocation.

ZebPay’s research-backed process may also update allocations or tokens when market conditions or a
coin’s status changes.

Open Rebalancing History to review what changed and when. You can also see the relevant transaction
details, standard rebalancing fees, and applicable taxes.

Rebalancing can be enabled or disabled from your Pack settings, so the choice stays with you.
Review your CryptoPack and enable Rebalancing when you’re ready.
