## Intent

Create a playful, enthusiastic, trustworthy mobile product promo that uses real ZebPay app screens
to explain CryptoPack Rebalancing and finish on the Enable Rebalancing action. Visual fidelity and
product truth matter more than spectacle.

## Assets

- `../../../../Cryptopack Rebalacing In-App Marketing Video.pdf` — source brief and embedded app
  captures.
- `../../../../docs/scripts/01-sell-and-explain.md` — approved primary narration.
- `../../../../docs/claims-register.md` — working claim constraints.

## Customizations

- Build exact product motion deterministically from real screenshots.
- Use an enthusiastic female neutral-English ElevenLabs voice; start with one voice only.
- Add a restrained, light playful technology music bed and small UI accents.
- Burn readable captions for muted in-app playback.

## Notes

- Produce only the Sell + Explain script in this round; preserve the walkthrough and conversion
  scripts as documented alternatives.
- Do not use “optimal strategy,” “protect your long-term returns,” guaranteed-return language, or
  unsupported continuous-monitoring claims.
- Clearly label explanatory percentage drift as illustrative.
- Fresh app captures may replace the PDF-extracted images before picture lock, but they are not a
  blocker for the first review cut.
- The final remains a review cut until human brand, legal, regulatory, pronunciation, music-rights,
  and creative approval.
