ZebPay CryptoPack Rebalancing

Crypto markets never stand still. As prices move, the coins inside your CryptoPack can drift away
from their target allocation.

CryptoPack Rebalancing brings it back on track automatically—trimming coins that have become
overweight and buying those that are underweight.

ZebPay’s research-backed process may also update allocations or tokens as market conditions change,
helping your Pack stay aligned with its intended strategy.

Every adjustment stays transparent. Users can check Rebalancing History to see what changed and
when, plus standard fees and applicable taxes.

Users remain in control and may enable or disable rebalancing from the Pack experience.

Call to action: Enable Rebalancing.
