## Objective

Help an existing ZebPay CryptoPack user understand allocation drift and confidently choose
**Enable Rebalancing** after seeing how realignment, possible research-backed updates, history,
fees/taxes, and user control work.

## Audience and platforms

- Primary audience: existing ZebPay CryptoPack users.
- Viewing context: a MoEngage mobile in-app popup/nudge, potentially beginning muted.
- Format: 1080×1920 portrait, 30 fps, approximately 45 seconds.
- Language: neutral international English.

## Story, message, and CTA

- Message: market movements can shift a Pack away from its target allocation; rebalancing brings
  it back in line while changes remain transparent and controllable.
- Emotional turn: uncertainty about market drift becomes calm, informed control.
- CTA: **Enable Rebalancing**.

## Visual and audio direction

- Real app captures are the product truth.
- Deterministic motion clarifies allocation drift, realignment, research-backed updates, history,
  and control.
- Playful, enthusiastic female ElevenLabs narration; one voice in this round.
- Light playful technology bed, small UI sounds, and readable burned captions.

## Constraints and approvals

- Follow `../../docs/claims-register.md`.
- Illustrative allocation movement must be labelled.
- Do not fabricate UI, transactions, performance, fees, or feature behavior.
- Four PDF-extracted screenshots are the baseline. Current source captures may replace them before
  picture lock.
- Brand, legal, regulatory, pronunciation, music-rights, creative, and final-promotion approvals
