# Clear Gut, "Ingredient Army", SCRIPT (v3, disciplined)

**Format:** vertical 9:16, ~9 shots, ~60s. 3D-caricature (So Sorry / Krishna's-SHAPEFIX style).
**Core discipline (the director's rules):**
1. **One speaker per shot.** Dialogue shots are SOLO, only the speaking character on camera, talking to lens. (Veo moves every visible mouth, so two talkers = mush.)
2. **Multiple characters in frame → NARRATOR voiceover** (no character lip-sync).
3. **Good vs evil reads instantly:** villain = dark/gross/menacing; heroes = bright/warm/friendly; stomach = sympathetic victim. Evil faces for the King ONLY.
4. Dialogue verbatim in **Devanagari**; direction in English.

## Why this is better
- **Dialogue scenes (1-5) are single-speaker** → clean lip-sync, no "who's talking", each hero unmistakably a GOOD guy alone in warm light.
- **Group/abstract scenes (6-8) are narrated** → no multi-mouth problem at all.
- **Heroes look heroic, not villainous** (rule 3). The villain is the only menacing face.
- **Product is perfect** at the one moment that matters most, the Scene 9 real-bottle card, regardless of Veo's label drift.

## Cast & assigned voice tone (Veo native, described in prompt)
King = deep, gravelly, comically-villainous · Amla = warm confident young-hero · Ajwain = bright, fast, energetic · Saunf = smooth, calm, gentle **FEMALE** · Haritaki = deep, wise, kindly-elder (warm not menacing) · Narrator = warm, trustworthy, authoritative (same across 6/7/8).
