Characters first. Then scenes and story objects.

We created clean reference images for Rama, the squirrel, the Vanaras, and each Ram Setu construction state before asking for any moving shot.

Keep exact branding out. The ZebPay logo and written text are added later in post.

03 Create one image for each beat

Use the approved elements to make story keyframes.

Each keyframe is the exact moment the audience must understand. The same character and location references are reused; only action, composition, and bridge state change.

05 Set up Higgsfield Cinema Studio

Register the approved assets as reusable Elements.

We created project Elements once, then attached the right character and location to each shot instead of describing the visual world again from memory.

We did not create Prop Elements for this film. Pebbles and boulders stayed shot-level objects; the bow stayed with Rama; text and logo stayed in post.

06 Generate motion one beat at a time

Tell Seedance only what should move.

The start image already contains the art direction. The prompt adds one clear action, one camera move, stability rules, and native ambience or SFX.

Example: subtle work motion across the bridge; cloth and fur move naturally; one slow dolly forward; stable faces, hands, boulders, and framing. Audio: ocean surf, coastal wind, stone impacts. No dialogue.
01 · Story anchor

Approved start image

The exact keyframe is supplied as start_image, not a generic reference.

02 · Continuity

Character + location

Attach only the Elements actually visible in this shot.

03 · Motion

One action, one camera move

Avoid stacking multiple story changes into a four-second request.

04 · Test controls

Seedance 2.0 · 480p · 9:16

4–6 seconds, High bitrate, Sound On, one candidate.

Important continuity rule

Use the previous generated clip only for a true continuous shot. For a new composition, return to the approved character, location, and keyframe sources.

07 Review before retrying

Keep usable motion. Do not reroll blindly.

Every take is checked against the keyframe, character continuity, story direction, temporal stability, and audio. A broken ending does not erase a good opening.

Keeper

Use the clip as generated.

Partial

Use the valid range, then hold or cut before the failure.

Reject

Record the failure class and change one control only.

Clear ownership prevents late surprises

Two business teams. Three clean lanes.

Marketing owns

Meaning and market fit

  • Audience and campaign objective
  • Single promise and emotional tone
  • Call to action and brand voice
  • Creative acceptance
Product owns

Truth and product behavior

  • What the product actually does
  • Terms, acronyms, numbers, and claims
  • Regulatory and factual questions
  • Final product-language approval
Creative production owns

Craft, evidence, and delivery

  • Script adaptation and shot design
  • Continuity, generation, and editing
  • Cost packets and technical QC
  • Versioned review cuts and masters
The production team does not approve the campaign claim, and Product does not need to direct camera motion. Each team has a narrow, high-value decision surface.
The highest-leverage gate

The animatic is the first complete film.

Still frames, timed to narration, reveal story problems before they become expensive video problems.

Lock the spoken storyEvery sentence receives an exact time slot.
Design one frame per beatComposition and continuity become visible.
Watch the whole filmPacing, crop, transitions, and comprehension are tested together.
Approve before motionThe team signs off on meaning, not isolated pretty images.
Change words, timing, and shot order here. Do not discover those problems after paid generation.
An animatic is not a rough mood board. It is the full story with the exact voice timing. Ask the audience to imagine approving this before seeing any moving shots.

AI for performance. Code for precision.

Every take gets a verdict

Review before reroll.

A failed take is not automatically wasted. The useful part may still solve the shot.

Before another generation:

Can we use a shorter range, hold one frame, crop, retime, add a cutaway, or repair the sound in post?

PASS

The planned action, continuity, sound, and usable duration meet the written criteria.

FAIL

A specific visible problem blocks the shot. Change one diagnosed variable before retrying.

NEEDS HUMAN

Taste, pronunciation, brand tone, music choice, or legal judgment cannot be certified by technical checks.

The reference film salvaged a valid section from a take where a prop later disappeared. This saved a blind reroll and kept the final edit controlled.

Learning compounds

  • Folder structure, brief, phrase map, and shot tracker

Reusability is operational, not creative sameness. We reuse the rails and evidence, while the campaign story and visual world remain original.
What we need from the room

Output
